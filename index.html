The Artist

Tatiana Volkova

Tatiana Volkova is a self-taught contemporary artist with rich cultural roots in both Ukraine and Russia. Driven by a profound passion for creation, she has spent years meticulously honing her craft through independent study and online resources, translating her unique imagination directly onto the canvas.

Her portfolio is a deeply personal exploration of color, texture, and form, carefully designed to breathe life and emotion into any environment. Tatiana creates art with a singular purpose: to bring joy to others. She invites you to experience the world through her perspective and to collect her original paintings to elevate your home.

For those inspired to pick up a brush themselves, Tatiana also offers guidance, sharing her self-taught techniques so that others can learn the beautiful art of painting alongside her.
